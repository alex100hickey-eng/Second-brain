SPLIT SECOND - TWO MODES THAT DO NOT EXIST YET
Spec for the session that built the app. Neither of these is a section - both are new run modes.

WHY
The call-and-read mode answers WHICH. Some defensive movement is about WHEN, and some is about
HOW FAST. Those are different drills and the current engine cannot run either.

MODE 1 - GO CUE
A beep at an unpredictable moment while he is already moving. No spoken word, no call, no read.
  He is sliding when the rep starts. The beep is the only information.
  Delay to the beep must VARY REP TO REP and the range has to be wider than the current 0-3s
  jitter. Something like 3-12 seconds, uniform, re-rolled every rep.
  Rest between reps around 8-10 seconds, configurable - he needs to reset and start sliding again.
  Audio must be a tone, not speech. Speech telegraphs by its own onset envelope and he wants
  a pure startle cue.
USED BY - Flip and Go
  Slide in help position. On the beep, flip your hips and sprint to touch the block or the
  backboard. That is the whole rep. Trains the hip flip and the first three steps.

MODE 2 - GO CUE PLUS STOPWATCH
Same beep, plus timing.
  Beep fires at a varying delay, exactly as Mode 1.
  A stopwatch STARTS on the beep.
  He taps anywhere on the screen to STOP it when the rep is finished.
  The time shows immediately, next to his best time for that drill.
  Store per-drill best and last five. Beating the best is the whole point of the mode.
  He knows the drill before it starts - curl or flare is a setup decision, not a call. So this
  mode does not need calls at all. One drill, one movement, one time.
USED BY - Chase
  Cone as the off-ball screen. Set up for a curl OR a flare before the rep - you have to know
  which to set the drill up. On the beep, chase it, get low, push through the line, finish the
  movement, tap to stop.
  The stopwatch is the progression. There is no level ladder on this one.
NOTE - this mode is worth more than these two drills. Closeout Stop on the Defense page is the
same shape and would be better timed than levelled.

WHAT WORKS TODAY, NO APP CHANGE
Recover and Slide runs as a normal section right now - four calls, Left, Right, Left back,
Right back. Set rest to 8s or more.
