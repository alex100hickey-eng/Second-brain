1. SPIN SPIN BACK PULL UP                                     NOW: L1
Spin start, spin back, straight into a 1-dribble pull up. If it takes more than 6 attempts to beat a drill, next drill
  L1  8 in a row both sides
  L2  Beyond NBA 3
  L3  Fade out of bounds
  L4  Back it up more

2. ROLL AND PICK UP                                           NOW: L___
Start at nail, roll it fast - 4 in a row per side, cant miss more than 5              
  L1  Slide facing hoop - college 3
  L2 Slide back to hoop, pick up 180
  L4  Wait and sprint into a pickup (Curl)
  L5  Wait and sprint into a pickup (Quick 180)
  L6  Wait and sprint into a pickup (Quick 180 fadeaway)
  L7  Wait and sprint into a pickup (Curl Fadeaway)
  L8 Back L4-7 up

3. LOSE THE RIM                                               NOW: L___
High bounce at half, back pedal, 180. make 8 in a row cant miss more than 6
  L1  Catch facing rim, rip it
  L2  Catch facing rim, No dip
  L3  Beyond NBA 3
  L4  Highpoint ball, 180 in air, rip it
  L5  Highpoint ball, 180 in air, no dip
  L6  Back it up

4. SPRINT INTO IT                                             NOW: L___
Start at nail, High bounce, touch the paint, sprint into a curl, shoot on arrival. 8 in a row alternating sides ant miss more than 6
  L1  College 3
  L2  NBA 3
  L3  Beyond NBA 3
  L4  Fully fade
  L5  Get deeper
