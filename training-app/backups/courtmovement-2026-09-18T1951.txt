MOVEMENT - DRIVE POSITION
Morning, 10 min, no ball. Low = low in stride: front shin forward, back leg long, chest tall, front heel down.
Rotate A, B, C.   LAST: ___

Deep Split Stance Hold - 2x20s ea
Low Stride Walk - 2x10 strides, hold the bottom of each 2s
Wall Drive Hold - 2x15s ea
Ankle Rock in Stride - 2x8 ea

A - DECELERATION - rest 45s
3-Step Drive to 2-Step Stop - 4x ea
3-Step Drive to 1-Step Stop - 4x ea
Stop and Go, Same Direction - 3x ea
Stop and Go, Opposite Direction - 3x ea
Stop on the Inside Foot - 3x ea
Stop on the Outside Foot - 3x ea
Hold every stop 2s. A hop or a second foot to catch it does not count.

B - DISSOCIATION - rest 45s
Arc Run, Chest to the Rim - 4x ea way
Crossover Step to Re-Plant - 4x8 ea
Drop Step and Open Hip Run - 4x ea
Snake Run Through 4 Cones, Shoulders Square - 4x ea way
Zig-Zag Drive Stride, Torso Still - 3x ea way
Hips turn. Chest stays quiet.

C - OFF AXIS AND RHYTHM - rest 45s
Single-Leg Landing from an Angle - 3x3 ea
Off-Balance Recovery Step - 3x5 ea
Half Speed to Full Speed to Stop - 4x ea
Full Speed to Half Speed to Full Speed - 4x ea
Stutter to Burst - 4x ea
Stick every landing 2s, second foot never touches.

PROGRESS
A - shorten the stop, never lengthen the run-in. B - faster, same quiet chest. C - steeper angle, less warning.
Daily holds - add 5s when the last rep looks like the first, cap 30s.

[Court movement] Defense
======================================================================
DEFENSE
25-30 min, after the bag. Nothing here is scored on makes. 2-3 drills a session, levels per drill.
Hips come up, the rep is over.

1. CLOSEOUT STOP                                              NOW: L___
Sprint at the target, stop on a dime, hold it, live with what comes next.
  L1  From 10 ft, stop on two feet, hold 2s
  L2  From the arc
  L3  Full sprint from half court
  L4  Stop on ONE foot and hold
  L5  Stop, then contain - three slides, no rise
  L6  Direction called late, as you arrive
  L7  Arrive on a bad angle, off balance, still stop
  L8  After conditioning

2. STAY LOW THROUGH THE ACTION                                NOW: L___
Four chairs as screens and bodies. Navigate the path without your hips rising once. Tape a mark at stance height.
  L1  Walk the path low
  L2  Game speed
  L3  Eyes up on a fixed point
  L4  Tighter path
  L5  A direction change at every chair
  L6  Height standard the entire way, no exceptions
  L7  Tired

3. ALL WAYS                                                   NOW: L___
Slide, sprint, backpedal, open-hip run, re-plant. The transitions between them are the skill.
  L1  Four modes in a fixed order
  L2  Random order
  L3  Called late, already moving
  L4  Full speed
  L5  No stutter step between modes, ever
  L6  A chair mid-sequence
  L7  Tired

4. BEATEN AND BACK                                            NOW: L___
Start already beaten. Re-accelerate, take the angle to the SPOT, get back in front.
  L1  Start at his hip
  L2  Start a full step beaten
  L3  Start turned the wrong way
  L4  Start with your back to the ball - find it, then go
  L5  Recovery angle called late
  L6  From a dead stop
  L7  Start further beaten every session

DEFENSE READS - SPLIT SECOND
All built and live in the app. Full spec: training-app/split-second-defense.json.

ON BALL - contain, beat him to the spot, hip to hip, no rise

BALL SCREEN D - say your coverage out loud before the rep
  Come off / Reject / Snake / Split / Pull up / Re-screen

TRANSITION D - sprint back, know who you have before you arrive
  Ball / Rim / Deepest / Match / Trailer

RECOVER AND SLIDE - slide, touch the floor, sprint back, go the way the call says
  Left / Right / Left back / Right back

FLIP AND GO - GO CUE mode. Slide in help, beep, flip the hips, sprint to the block or the backboard.
CHASE - GO CUE + STOPWATCH. Set up curl or flare, beep, chase it, tap to stop. Race your own best time.

Closeout D was cut - Recover and Slide covers it. The braking half is Closeout Stop on the Defense page.

[Court movement] RECORDING - Does It Sell
======================================================================
RECORDING - DOES IT SELL
Sun, Mon, Fri, Sat. 10-15 min, END of the workout, never cold.
Camera in FRONT of you at defender height. Tripod, slow-mo, tape the camera spot and your start spot.
3-4 moves, 3 reps each. About a dozen clips, all watched back.

Every clip gets one verdict:
SELLS - a frame where the defender has to commit wrong
DOESN'T SELL - nothing in it would have moved him
CAN'T TELL - too slow, or the camera was in the wrong place

One BEST REP clip per move. Beat it, replace it. Same angle every time.
Didn't sell = the move you work next session.

[Court movement] Film Protocol
======================================================================
FILM PROTOCOL
First session of every month, before the session, fresh. Same time of day every month.
Tripod, never handheld. Slow-mo, 240fps if you have it. Landscape, full body, hip height.
Tape the camera spot and your start spot. Leave the tape.

THE SEQUENCE - always this order, no warm-up reps in the take
1. Split stance hold, side view - 10s ea
2. Low stride walk, side view - 4 strides
3. 3-step drive to 1-step stop, side view - 2 ea
4. Zig-zag drive stride, front view - 2 ea way
5. Closeout to 3 slides, baseline view - 2 ea
6. Single-leg landing from an angle, front view - 2 ea
7. Drive and kick pass, baseline view - 2 ea

One folder per month, named by date. Never delete one - the old months are the baseline.
Analysis is not built. Film anyway - it runs over old months later. Months you skip are gone.
